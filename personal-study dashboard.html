🌱 Gentle revision strategy

  1. Review class notes briefly on the same day.
  2. Practise questions without looking at worked solutions.
  3. Mark your answers and record mistakes.
  4. Revisit difficult questions after a few days.
  5. Use timed past-paper practice once the relevant content is covered.
