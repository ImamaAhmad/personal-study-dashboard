# My Study Diary

*A little progress every day is still progress :)*

**Live Website:** [My Study Diary](https://personal-study-dashboard.netlify.app/)

## About the Project

My Study Diary is a pastel-themed study planner I created to organise my daily routine, manage revision, and work towards my goal of achieving four A*s at A-level.

I wanted a study space that feels calm, personal, and motivating: somewhere to plan my days, track my progress, and remind myself that small steps matter.

The website is built using HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript in a single HTML file, making it simple to run, customise, and maintain.

## Features

- **Home Dashboard:** View daily goals, study sessions, focused minutes, and reminders.
- **Weekly Timetable:** Organise school lessons, travel, study time, meals, and rest.
- **Tasks and Goals:** Create tasks, set priorities and due dates, and track daily goals.
- **Revision Tracker:** Track revision topics across Mathematics, Physics, Economics, and Computer Science.
- **Reminder Checklist:** Keep track of personal medicine reminders.
- **Pomodoro Timer:** Customisable focus sessions, breaks, and full-screen mode.
- **Rain Ambience:** Generate relaxing rain-like audio locally in the browser.
- **A-level Progress Tracker:** Record assessed grades and work towards an A* in each subject.
- **Exam Practice Log:** Record marks, track results, and reflect on mistakes.
- **Study Statistics:** Monitor completed sessions, focused time, and study streaks.
- **Backup and Restore:** Export diary data and restore it from a backup file.
- **Pastel Design:** Soft colours, rounded cards, and a calm, cosy atmosphere.

## Built With

- **HTML5** — Website structure
- **CSS3** — Styling, layouts, and responsive design
- **Vanilla JavaScript** — Interactive features, timers, and progress tracking
- **Local Storage** — Saving diary data in the browser
- **Web Audio API** — Generating rain ambience locally

No frontend frameworks or build tools are required.

## How to Use

1. Visit the [live website](https://personal-study-dashboard.netlify.app/).
2. Explore the different sections using the navigation menu.
3. Organise your timetable and daily tasks.
4. Track your revision and exam-practice results.
5. Review your progress and adjust your study goals.

## Data and Privacy

The diary stores information in your browser's local storage.

- Your saved data is generally available when you reopen the website in the same browser and website origin.
- Your diary does not automatically synchronise between devices.
- Clearing browser data may remove your saved information.
- Use the **Download my diary backup** option in the Progress section to export your data regularly.
